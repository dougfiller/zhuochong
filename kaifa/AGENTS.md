# Product planning and acceptance instructions

- `最终需求文档.md` is the formal product baseline. Keep safety and privacy constraints intact; substantive baseline changes require an explicit version/date update and authorization.
- `kaifa_plan/` records ordered implementation plans, `kaifa_log/` records completed work, `kaifa_personnel/` contains process guidance, and `kaifa_test/` contains gates and fixtures. Do not blur plans, claims, and evidence.
- Preserve timestamped historical records. Add a new superseding record instead of silently rewriting an old review or conclusion.
- Do not claim a milestone complete from source presence alone. Link claims to applicable tests, real evidence, candidate identity, and gate status.
- Never include real chat content, credentials, signing material, private database paths, or personally identifying screenshots in plans, logs, or fixtures.
- Reference projects and visual assets remain read-only and non-distributable until their license/authorization is recorded.
